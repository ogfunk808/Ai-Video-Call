Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Your privacy is central to our values. This Privacy Policy details how Aetheris collects, processes, and protects your information, including camera stream analysis, microphone recording data, and token usage statistics.

1. Data We Do Not Store

Unlike standard call providers, Aetheris processes video streams and audio inputs in memory directly on the client side using the HTML5 Web Audio API and WebRTC local loopbacks. We do NOT record or save your webcam video frame-by-frame on remote database servers. All real-time synthesis remains transient.

2. Information We Collect

We collect structural parameters to optimize local performance:

  • Token Usage: Call duration times, token counts, and token replenishment cycles are stored locally in secure browser storage (localStorage) to verify daily allocations.
  • Performance Telemetry: Estimated fps, browser media streams layout, local network latency, and selected AI mood targets are used to adjust Canvas visualizer response curves.

3. AI Processing & Speech Data

When you speak to the avatar, microphone audio is analyzed using local FFT spectral nodes to render visual feedback. Speech-to-text transcription occurs dynamically and locally. Transcripts generated during the session are held in transient memory and deleted immediately upon ending the call.

4. Security of Media Feeds

We utilize browser sandbox features to isolate WebRTC camera tracks. Any media interaction is conducted within strict HTTPS context constraints to prevent visual hacking or frame injection.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

Aetheris does not sell, trade, or distribute your behavioral telemetry, token history, or chat transcripts to advertising firms or metadata consolidators.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke microphone or camera access at any time through your browser's security settings. Clearing your browser's cache will reset all local storage data, including token configurations.
